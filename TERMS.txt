PRIVACY
=======

This explains what the program stores on your PC and what it sends over the
internet.

Kept on your PC
  * History (recognitions.csv): artist, title, album, ISRC code, time, which
    service matched, BPM and key (and whether they were looked up or
    estimated), links, and the web address of the cover art.
  * last_clip.wav: your most recent captured clip, overwritten each time. Set
    SAVE_LAST_CLIP=0 to disable.
  * retry_clip.wav: a clip ShazamIO missed, kept only until you retry, dismiss
    it, or a track is matched.
  * .env: your settings, including any API key. Treat it like a password.
  * whatsplaying.log: technical messages from the program.
  * Deleting History rows removes them from the list and recognitions.csv, but
    a .bak.csv backup made just before still holds them until you delete it.

No tracking by the author

The program has no analytics or telemetry. The author does not receive your
history, audio or any other data.

What is sent over the internet
  * Every identification: ShazamIO builds an audio fingerprint from the clip
    on your PC and sends it to Shazam's servers (run by Apple), together with
    the usual details any internet request reveals, such as your IP address.
  * Only if you click Try shazam-api.com: the audio clip itself is uploaded to
    shazam-api.com using your key. Its privacy policy says uploaded audio is
    deleted after processing, generally within an hour, and that it logs
    request details such as IP address and time.
  * Only if you add a GetSongBPM key: the artist name and track title (text
    only, no audio) are sent to GetSongBPM, with the usual details such as
    your IP address, to look up BPM and key. Estimating BPM and key from the
    clip happens entirely on your PC.
  * Find on links: clicking one opens your default browser at that site's
    search page, with the artist and title in the address. That site then sees
    your visit and its own privacy policy applies.
  * Cover art: the window loads images from web addresses supplied by those
    services, so the image hosts can see your IP address.

Take care with sensitive audio

The program records everything your PC plays, so avoid running it during
calls, meetings or anything private, especially if other people's voices could
be captured.

Their policies apply too

Once data reaches those services, their own privacy policies apply, not this
one.

Your choices

Delete History rows in the app, delete the files above, or delete the
program's folder to remove everything. Uninstalling deliberately keeps .env
and recognitions.csv so you do not lose them by accident.
  shazam-api.com privacy policy: https://shazam-api.com/privacy
  Apple privacy information: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  GetSongBPM website (see its own policy): https://getsongbpm.com
